# Human Decision Semantics

Hi distinguishes `AUTHORITY`, `PREFERENCE`, `AMBIGUITY`, `ANNOTATION`, `VISUAL_DECISION`, and `BATCHED_QUESTIONS`. A question is asked only when the answer can materially change correctness, scope, preference, contract, security, irreversible effect, or authority.

Independent material questions may be batched; dependent questions are sequenced. Active user messages are classified as `INTERRUPT`, `QUEUE`, or `SIDEBAND` so side questions do not destructively rewrite the mission.
